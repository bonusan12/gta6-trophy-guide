Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 difficulty and around 200 hours, in a single playthrough. There are no online trophies, no difficulty settings and no mission replay, and free-roam carries on after the story. Cheats switch trophies off, so leave them alone. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots). The stages below are built from the official trophy descriptions.

⚠ Just 1 trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate on the balcony with Lucia at Jason’s house (after Down to Earth) before Chapter 1 ends. Everything else can be finished after the story in free-roam.

      Sources & honesty note

      Official trophy names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them (listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, checked 20 Nov 2026). Every guide above is written in our own words for this entry and names its source beside it — a source is cited, never copied.

        What is not confirmed yet: the game only came out on 19 November 2026, so a few methods are still being confirmed by the public guides. Positive Mental Attitude is the one potentially missable trophy and it is unconfirmed whether its Chapter 1 meditation counts; Fair Game’s exact animal is unconfirmed; The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin are reported methods still being confirmed. Those four guides say so in plain language — nothing here presents an unconfirmed method as fact, and no trophy name or unlock method is invented.
